PROSPECTIVE PROTEIN CONTACT BENCHMARK — FROZEN RULES 1.1

Purpose
Test the binary expected-median construction associated with OpenAI/math #332
against strong conventional controls on additional protein simulations. The
reference is an uncorrupted simulation, not experimental biological truth.
The earlier adenylate-kinase result is development information only.

Data and sealed splits
T4 lysozyme: exact deposited mutant construct; md1us4 discovery, md1us5
validation, md1us6/7/8 test. Verify every original file's published MD5.
Ubiquitin: use only if native identity, time ordering, five run boundaries and
unbiased production provenance are documented. Otherwise report qualification
failure. For verified runs, original run order gives one discovery, one
validation and three test runs. No silent replacement dataset.
Villin HP35: separate adaptive stress test. Archive group 1 discovery, group 2
validation, group 3 test. Groups are putative campaigns, with independence not
certified. Within each group choose trajectories by ascending SHA256 of archive
path, capped at 24 discovery, 24 validation and 48 test. No motion-based selection.

Inputs and features
Sample every 200 ps without interpolation. Extract all CA atoms, including
nonstandard protein residues; make bonded proteins whole if needed. Use strict
CA distance <8 Angstrom and sequence separation >=4. Sensitivities use 7/9
Angstrom. Only discovery-variable pairs select primary features. Report how
many held-out positive contacts that selection omits. Never join trajectories.
Rare means discovery contact frequency <=10%. Brief means complete positive
runs of at most 600 ps (three sampled frames), excluding boundary-censored runs.

Methods and tuning
Raw contacts; linear resolvent; #332 cubic; known-error-rate corrected linear
oracle; hard threshold; moving average; Gaussian filter; temporal median;
power sharpening. Seven smoothing settings per basic method; full power uses
63 settings. A separate matched comparison fixes alpha=3 from ADK development
and uses seven settings. Exact grids, boundaries and tie order are in JSON.
Every method has identical validation access. Choose the strongest nonoracle
baseline by validation only, including raw. Save choices before test evaluation.

Noise and checks
Primary: independent 10% binary flips. Secondary: 5% and 20% flips; Gaussian
coordinate perturbation of 0.25/0.50 Angstrom; temporally correlated 10% flips
with lag-one correlation 0.8; the 7/9 Angstrom contact cutoffs. Apply settings
selected under primary noisy validation to clean data to measure distortion.
Eight validation and sixteen test noise draws per trajectory, with fixed,
paired seed derivation in JSON. No test tuning or favorable-scenario selection.

Primary T4 decision
Average each run over noise draws, then weight the three held-out runs equally.
Practical success requires ALL of:
1. At least 2% lower mean Brier error than the validation-selected full-budget
   nonoracle baseline.
2. Strictly lower Brier error on each of the three held-out runs.
3. No more than two percentage points lost in rare-positive-cell recall on
   any held-out run.
4. No more than two percentage points lost in brief-positive-cell recall on
   any held-out run.
Missing strata make the composite criterion not fully evaluable. These are
predeclared engineering thresholds, not consequences of a mathematical theorem.
Ubiquitin replication is separate; villin cannot alter the T4 primary decision.

Reporting and verification
Report every prespecified scenario, every run and method, feature coverage,
clean-input damage, settings, runtime, hashes, environment and deviations.
Noise-only confidence intervals describe fixed-trajectory corruption variation;
they do not imply biological replication. Five independent AI agents will audit
mathematics, data/provenance, statistics, citations/claims, and reproduction.
This is a locally frozen prospective protocol, not a public registry filing.

The accompanying JSON is the authoritative complete executable specification.
