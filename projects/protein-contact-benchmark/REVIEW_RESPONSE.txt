RESPONSE TO INDEPENDENT TECHNICAL AUDITS

This record documents findings and resolutions. The five reviewers are separate
AI agents with distinct audit roles; they are not human peer reviewers or
independent experimental laboratories. Initial records are preserved unchanged.
Final review records consolidate both completed benchmark datasets and the
separate ubiquitin qualification decision.

1. Citation accuracy and claim scope
The NumPy/SciPy reference lists now explicitly say et al. where abbreviated.
Conference venues and series are retained. The PDB structure's actual depositors
are credited and the repository is identified separately. UniProt P00720 has an
explicit source URL and access date. The report distinguishes generic controls
from related TimeScapes literature and does not claim to reproduce TimeScapes or
MixMatch. Unused Jaeger/Wolf context was removed from the final bibliography.
The endpoint construction is explicitly an expectation over three independent
geometric-walk endpoints. The released inequality is not a guarantee of lower
Brier error, and the cubic transformation predates the new proof.

2. Generic uncertainty wording in immutable Villin JSON
Some Villin result JSON interpretation fields inherit the shared protocol phrase
about three simulation runs. Those three test runs belong to T4. Villin instead
uses 48 selected dependent adaptive trajectories from a single held-out archive
group, including 47 paths of 250 sampled frames and one path of 71 frames. Final
reporting uses that dataset-specific description. Original result files are
preserved; this note corrects the explanatory wording, not numerical values.
Neither noise replicates nor individual contact cells are biological replicates.

3. Floating-point threshold ties
The mathematics reviewer used an independent spectral resolvent implementation.
Its continuous predictions agreed to numerical precision. In a sampled Villin
5%-flip comparison, seven distinct near-0.5 cells (appearing in both equivalent
power families) fell on opposite sides of the >=0.5 convention under the two
solvers, at distances of at most 1.14e-14 from 0.5. Raw discrepancies and the
reviewer's explicit tie alignment are retained in the audit. This is not exact
independent agreement of all classification metrics. The prescribed production
algorithm and its complete fresh-environment rerun agree exactly. Continuous
error estimates and the primary conclusion are unaffected by those secondary
scenario tie effects. The final T4 spectral audit also records three sampled
near-0.5 classification differences; its maximum continuous prediction errors
are below 7e-15. Neither dataset required a production threshold change.
The reproduction reviewer additionally traced two primary md1us6/noise15 cells: an independent Thomas solver yields exactly0.5, while DCT gives values just below0.5. Corresponding precision/recall discrepancies are at most2.11e-6 in that check, and the Thomas metrics match the recorded metrics within2e-12. Across that reviewer's sampled noisy and clean checks,119 unique run/draw cells are rounding-sensitive (some recur in several method comparisons). The largest raw spectral metric shift is0.00055826 in clean-input rare-contact recall, or0.0558 percentage points. The primary failure already follows from the much larger continuous Brier gap. No threshold tolerance was added to production after observing outcomes.

4. SciPy temporal-median edge case outside the analyzed inputs
The mathematics reviewer reproduced anomalous scipy.ndimage.median_filter
behavior for some very short multicolumn inputs and large reflecting windows,
including two-frame examples. All declared window widths matched independent
manual repeated-reflection medians for tested actual-size inputs N=71,249,250,
and5001. No analyzed trajectory has fewer than 20 sampled frames; actual selected
Villin lengths are 71/250 and T4 lengths are 5001. The unused dependency edge case
was documented without changing the frozen benchmark implementation.

5. Villin cadence tolerance
The source extraction code uses a looser cadence tolerance than the frozen
preparation rule. The independently re-read raw archive has exact 100-ps native
spacing and exact 200-ps prepared spacing; every selected path satisfies the
stricter preparation rule. No data selection or output changes were required.

6. T4 preparation integrity hardening before T4 outcomes
The data reviewer identified that preparation did not require an extraction
completion manifest or compare its coordinate-file hash. Before any T4 contact
preparation, these checks were added, together with cross-checks against saved
published source checksums, geometry, frame count, atom count and cadence. The
original preparation script used for Villin is preserved under protocol/, with
both hashes and the dated reason in preparation_integrity_hardening.json. The
Villin branch, frozen scientific rules and numerical benchmark code are unchanged.

7. Source acquisition and coordinate reconstruction
The initial T4 extraction completed its coordinates but encountered a file-size
property/call error while writing metadata. The issue was corrected, and all
2,430,486 coordinate values in the first run matched re-extraction exactly.
Backbone-only periodic reconstruction likewise matched full-protein reconstruction
bitwise on that complete run. A later download reached a disk limit; obsolete
incomplete transport copies were removed and source-reader file handles explicitly
closed. Only successfully recorded byte ranges were reused. A complete published
MD5 and source-size check remains mandatory before every extraction; no partial
trajectory is accepted. Acquisition logs and the interruption record are retained.

8. Reproduction scope and reporting limits
Fresh dependency environments provide reproducibility on this host, not validation
on a different machine, operating system, laboratory or force field. Independent
reviewers additionally reconstructed coordinates, contact matrices, mathematical
operators, selections, summary metrics and uncertainty calculations within their
recorded scopes. Conditional noise-only intervals are pointwise and unadjusted
for multiple comparisons. All prespecified secondary scenarios are retained.
The original freeze was prospective and private, not a public preregistration.
